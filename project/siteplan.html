Site Name

Name: SecretSpots Guide

Reasoning: I chose this name because it directly targets travelers and locals looking for unique, lesser-known, and non-mainstream spots that aren't highlighted in typical mainstream tourist brochures.

Site Purpose

The purpose of SecretSpots Guide is to serve as an interactive, informational hub showcasing unique local cafes, quiet nature parks, and historic landmarks. The platform empowers users to break away from traditional tourist traps by providing a responsive, easy-to-use directory where locations can be filtered dynamically by category.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: As a local resident looking for a quiet place to read on the weekend, how can I find a secluded park or a calm, non-chain cafe nearby?
  • Scenario 2: As an adventurous traveler spending three days in the city, where can I find historic landmarks or architectural sites that aren't overcrowded with massive tour groups?

Color Schema

The chosen colors reflect a calm, earthy, and inviting exploration tone:

  • Primary Color (#1e4620 - Forest Green): Used for headers, main navigation backgrounds, and primary action buttons.
  • Accent Color (#d97706 - Amber/Gold): Used for interactive elements like active filters, hover states, and highlighting key recommendations.
  • Background Color (#fef3c7 - Warm Cream): Used as the main body background to keep text highly legible and easy on the eyes.
  • Text Color (#1f2937 - Dark Charcoal): Used for all standard body text and paragraphs to provide strong color contrast.

Typography

We will use a maximum of two clean, web-safe modern fonts to establish strong visual hierarchy:

  • Heading Font (Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif): Clean and bold, used for all <h1>, <h2>, and <h3> elements to stand out.
  • Body Font (Georgia, serif): An elegant serif style used for paragraphs, lists, and location card descriptions to create a high-quality editorial aesthetic.
